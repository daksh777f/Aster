## When You Need a Commercial License

A Commercial License is required if you:

- Integrate Aster into a **proprietary product or service** without releasing
  your source code under the AGPLv3
- Offer Aster as part of a **managed service or SaaS offering** to third parties
- Distribute Aster as part of an **OEM or white-label product**
- Require contractual **warranty, indemnification, or SLA** terms beyond what
  the AGPLv3 provides

A Commercial License is **not required** if you:

- Self-host Aster for internal use within your organization
- Use Aster in an open-source project licensed under the AGPLv3
- Evaluate Aster for non-production purposes
